# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-17  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Statutory Basis:** Turkish Penal Code (Cybercrimes Art. 243 et seq.), Law No. 5651, Intellectual Property Law No. 5846, Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, GDPR

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") applies to all users, visitors, and registered members of the Operis Platform ("System"). Its purpose is to safeguard platform security, cybersecurity infrastructure integrity, encrypted proposal architecture, and the professional engineering environment from misuse, unlawful acts, and cyberattacks.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Activities and Content

Carrying out or publishing the following activities and content types on the Platform is strictly prohibited:

1. **Malicious Software and Cyberattack Projects:**
   - Developing, distributing, commissioning, or purchasing malware (trojans, spyware, ransomware, keyloggers).
   - Conducting unauthorized penetration tests, exploit generation, vulnerability hunting, or cyberattack requests against third-party systems without formal, legally binding written authorization from the system owner. Only authorized defensive cybersecurity work is permitted.
   - Scripting or deploying botnets, DDoS tools, credential stuffing, or automated brute-force tools.
2. **Fraud, Counterfeit Finance, and Illicit Activities:**
   - Unlawful investment schemes, pyramid systems, cryptocurrency scams, or unlicensed gambling software.
   - Stolen credit cards, phishing frameworks, or account takeover requests.
   - Digital infrastructure requests pertaining to illicit substances, firearms, or contraband trade.
3. **Academic Dishonesty and Fraud:**
   - Commissioning or offering to complete academic assignments, theses, dissertations, capstone projects, or graded exams on behalf of others for submission to educational institutions.
4. **Intellectual Property and Copyright Violations:**
   - Copying, reverse-engineering, cracking, or unauthorized decompilation of proprietary third-party software, trade secrets, or confidential source code.
   - Distributing or selling proprietary assets without lawful license entitlement.
5. **Personal Data Violations and Doxxing:**
   - Disclosing national ID numbers, phone numbers, physical addresses, private correspondences, or sensitive personal data of third parties in listings or proposals without lawful consent.
6. **Circumvention and Platform Integrity Interference:**
   - Publicly posting unencrypted telephone numbers, email addresses, messaging links, or external payment gateways within project titles or descriptions to circumvent the encrypted bidding workflow and freshness radar.
   - Unauthorized scraping, crawling, automated harvesting, or indexing of platform data using unauthorized automated tools.
7. **Fake Listings and Market Manipulation:**
   - Publishing ghost or phantom listings without a bona fide engineering project or allocated budget solely to scout market pricing, solicit free architecture, or harvest developer ideas.
   - Operating multiple synthetic accounts to stage Sybil bidding attacks, artificial quote manipulation, or collusive bid suppression.

---

## 3. Violation Detection and Enforcement Sanctions

In the event of a breach of platform policies, Operis reserves the unilateral right to enforce the following remedial measures immediately:

1. **Immediate Content Takedown:** Listings, proposals, or profile entries violating these terms will be removed or taken down immediately without prior notice.
2. **Account Suspension or Permanent Termination:** The account of the offending User may be suspended temporarily or terminated permanently. Re-registration by the same individual under an alias is prohibited.
3. **Network-Level and IP Blocking:** IP addresses, ASNs, and device fingerprints identified as origins of attacks, bots, or severe policy breaches are permanently blacklisted at the Web Application Firewall (WAF) tier.
4. **Referral to Law Enforcement:** Conduct constituting criminal offenses under penal laws (e.g., unauthorized access to information systems, system impairment or data destruction, qualified fraud) is referred without delay to public prosecutors, specialized cybercrime directorates, and regulatory bodies.

---

## 4. User Liability and Full Indemnification

Users who engage in prohibited activities and cause damages to the system or third parties bear full, direct legal, administrative, penal, and civil liability. The User agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Operis against all damages, administrative fines, court costs, and legal fees incurred as a consequence of their breach, payable immediately upon first written demand.
