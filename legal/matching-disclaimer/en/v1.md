# Matching, Payment, and Liability Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-17  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Legal Status:** Non-Payment Institution Status Under Law No. 6493, Liability Disclaimer Under Turkish Code of Obligations Art. 115, and Covenant Not to Sue  
**Statutory Basis:** Law on Payment and Securities Settlement Systems No. 6493, Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 (Art. 115, 128, 520), Law No. 6563 Art. 9, Law No. 5651 Art. 5

---

## 1. Does Not Receive or Hold Funds

1. **Non-Payment Institution Declaration:** The Operis Platform is **strictly not** a bank, payment service provider, electronic money issuer, or escrow depository under Law No. 6493 and Central Bank regulations.
2. **Zero Escrow and No Fund Handling:** No cash payments, credit card processing, digital wallet storage, escrow holding, or fund freezing take place through the platform. Operis never handles, holds, routes, or touches transaction funds.
3. **Financial Disconnection:** All monetary settlements, milestone advances, progress payments, and invoices between the Employer and Contractor are executed directly through their independently chosen banking channels (IBAN, Wire, EFT, FAST, SWIFT) without platform intervention.
4. **Non-Involvement in Payment Disputes:** In instances where an Employer fails to pay or demands unjustified refunds, or where a Contractor receives payment but defaults on delivery, Operis bears zero financial, civil, or penal liability. Users irrevocably acknowledge that they cannot petition Operis for debt collection or financial compensation.

---

## 2. Is Not Party to Service Contracts

1. **Legal Character of Matching:** Offer acceptance or matching by an Employer constitutes solely a **Bilateral Handshake** that unlocks direct, encrypted communication channels between two independent professionals.
2. **Exclusion as Contractual Party:** Upon matching, counterparties establish an independent, bilateral legal relationship. Operis is not a party, guarantor, surety, agent, performance assistant, or inspector of this relationship, deliverable quality, or milestone completion.
3. **P2P Contract Draft Disclaimer:** Sample freelance service agreements and non-disclosure agreement (NDA) drafts downloadable or viewable within collaboration workspaces are provided strictly as **informational reference templates** to assist counterparties in self-protecting their rights. These templates do not constitute formal legal counsel; counterparties must review, customize, and accept all legal consequences of their contracts independently.

---

## 3. Does Not Adjudicate Commercial Disputes

1. **Platform is Not an Arbitrator:** In technical, commercial, financial, or penal disputes between users, Operis possesses no status as an arbitrator, arbitration board, court, expert witness, or notary.
2. **Direct Recourse Between Parties:** In the event of a dispute, counterparties must pursue claims and remedies directly and exclusively against one another before competent judicial or mediation authorities.

## 4. Software Quality, Vulnerabilities, and Defect Disclaimers

1. **Zero Warranty:** Operis provides no technical or legal warranty that deliverables, source codes, architectures, or database schemas delivered by Contractors are functional, bug-free, commercially viable, or free from vulnerabilities (backdoors, zero-day exploits, malware, SQL injection).
2. **Client Inspection Duty:** Inspecting deliverables, performing source code audits, and running vulnerability assessments and penetration tests prior to acceptance or production deployment is the sole and exclusive responsibility of the Client.
