## 2. Processed Personal Data and Privacy by Design

Rooted in the principle of data minimization, the Platform processes only the minimum personal data indispensable for secure service delivery:

1. **Identity Data:** Full legal name, date of birth (utilized solely to verify 18+ age majority).
2. **Contact Data:** City and country of residence, verified email address, verified mobile phone number (formatted according to E.164 international standard).
3. **Technical and Security Data:** One-way salted Scrypt password hashes, SHA-256 digital consent hashes, session tokens, IP addresses, statutory telecommunication access logs under Law No. 5651, and transaction timestamps.
4. **Data Strictly Never Collected:** National identity numbers (TCKN), government identity card photocopies, criminal background records, religious beliefs, trade union memberships, biometric data, or health records are **strictly never requested, collected, or stored** in our databases.

## 4. Cryptographic Security Architecture: AES-256-GCM & HMAC-SHA256

The Platform defends your personal data with military-grade cryptographic architectures:

1. **Application-Level Encryption (AES-256-GCM):** Sensitive database fields (names, mobile numbers) are encrypted at the application layer using **AES-256-GCM** prior to database persistence. Physical database breaches yield unreadable ciphertext without master keys.
2. **Blind Indexing (HMAC-SHA256):** Mobile number uniqueness and account deduplication are evaluated cryptographically using keyed HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing without storing or querying cleartext numbers.
3. **Encrypted Blind Proposals:** Contractor proposal fees and scope descriptions are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; competing developers can never view competing bids.
4. **Non-Reversible Password Security:** Passwords are never stored in cleartext or reversible formats; they are protected via one-way salted Scrypt key derivation functions.

## 5. Third-Party Transfers and Absolute Prohibition on Data Sales

1. **Zero Data Monetization:** The Platform strictly never sells, leases, licenses, or monetizes personal data to third-party advertising brokers, marketers, or analytics agencies.
2. **Match-Gated Progressive Disclosure:** Contact details (phone numbers, emails) remain sealed until an Employer confirms an offer. Upon mutual matching, contact details are disclosed solely between the two matched parties for contract execution.
3. **Authorized Public Authorities:** Data is disclosed to public prosecutorial bodies or judicial courts exclusively in response to binding, written, and lawful court subpoenas within statutory limits.

## 6. Data Retention and Erasure Policy

Personal data is retained during active membership and following account termination for the general 10-year statutory limitation period under commercial and contract laws to serve as evidence in potential disputes. Telecommunication logs under Law No. 5651 are preserved for the statutory 2-year duration. Upon expiration of mandatory periods, data is irreversibly erased, destroyed, or anonymized during regular periodic purge cycles.
