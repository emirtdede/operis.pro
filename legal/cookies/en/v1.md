**Statutory Basis:** Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (KVKK), Data Protection Board Guidelines on Cookies, EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC), GDPR

## 1. General Information Regarding Cookies

This Cookie Policy explains the types of cookies utilized across the web platform and digital interfaces operated by **Vellium** ("Company", developer and operator of the Operis platform; Operis is a proprietary product of Vellium) and describes how users may manage their preferences.

Cookies are compact text files stored on your terminal device (computer, tablet, smartphone) by your web browser when visiting a digital platform. Cookies are utilized to guarantee session security, preserve user configurations, and optimize system rendering.

---

## 2. Categories of Cookies Deployed on Our Platform

Pursuant to its strict privacy-by-design architecture, Operis **STRICTLY DOES NOT DEPLOY third-party advertising, commercial remarketing, behavioral tracking, or profiling cookies**. Our platform utilizes exclusively technically essential and functional first-party cookies:

### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are technically indispensable for the secure operation and integrity of the Platform. Disabling them prevents authenticated access and session persistence:

- `fp_session`: Encrypted, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax authentication cookie utilized to verify user identity, maintain authenticated sessions, and protect against session hijacking attacks. (Preserved for session duration or up to 7 days).
- `__cf_bm` / `cf_clearance`: Security cookies deployed by the Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot mitigation layer to distinguish legitimate human requests from automated brute-force attacks and malicious scripts.

### B. Preference and Functional Cookies

These cookies preserve your interface configurations so you do not need to re-enter them upon each page navigation:

- `fp_theme`: Stores the user's interface appearance preference (Light, Dark, True Black) and prevents screen flickering (Flash of Unstyled Content) during initial render. (Retained for 1 year).
- `fp_locale`: Preserves the user's selected language option (Turkish or English). (Retained for 1 year).
- `fp_cookie_consent`: Records and maintains the user's explicit cookie category selection in local application storage.

## 3. Statutory Legal Grounds for Processing

Strictly necessary cookies are processed under the statutory legal grounds of *"direct necessity for the establishment or performance of a contract"* and *"legitimate interests of the data controller"* pursuant to Article 5(2)(c) and 5(2)(f) of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. Because no third-party tracking or behavioral advertising cookies are deployed, user browsing data is never transferred to commercial ad exchanges.

---

## 4. Managing and Disabling Cookies via Web Browsers

Users may review, delete, block, or configure automated alerts for cookies at any time by modifying their browser settings:

- **Google Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-Party Cookies and Site Data
- **Mozilla Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection & Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- **Microsoft Edge:** Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions > Manage and Delete Cookies

*Note: Blocking strictly necessary session cookies via browser controls will prevent authentication and access to the Operis user dashboard.*
