## 3. Statutory Hosting Provider and Intermediary Status

Vellium:
1. **Under Law No. 5651:** Operates as an official **"Hosting Provider"** (Yer Saglayici) registered under Information and Communication Technologies Authority ("BTK") regulations. The Company bears no statutory duty to investigate ex officio the legality of third-party content published by users.
2. **Under Law No. 6563:** Operates as an **"Electronic Commerce Intermediary Service Provider"** (Araci Hizmet Saglayici) under the regulatory authority of the Ministry of Trade.
3. **ETBIS Registration:** Registered in the Electronic Commerce Information System ("ETBIS") maintained by the Ministry of Trade.
